Built on Zerion CLI

Forked the CLI, then layered.

Zerion CLI is the execution engine — we route every swap through /swap/offers, sign locally with the user's key, and carry Zerion's default commission. On top of that we built per-user custody, credibility scoring, a Telegram front-end, and a DCA scheduler.

What it does

Four things, one bot.

01

Credibility scoring

Weighs realized PnL, overall win rate, asset-specific win rate, and recency. Auto-flags dust-inflated wallets (e.g. vitalik.eth shows −100% total but +10% realized — we score the honest signal).

02

One-tap mirror

When a watched wallet trades, you get a scored alert and a Mirror button. Tap it; bot swaps USDC → the asset on the same chain through your custodial wallet. Roughly 15 seconds end-to-end.

03

DCA on a schedule

/dca ETH 5 hourly base — $5 into ETH every hour on Base. Schedule persists in SQLite. Ticks that violate policy are skipped with a clear reason; the plan keeps running.

04

Scoped policies

Six policies on every swap: max $/trade, allowed chains, slippage cap, daily spend limit, cooldown, auto-mirror off by default. One checkPolicy() call gates everything.

Flow

Fund once, follow, tap.

  1. Step 01

    Generate wallets

    /wallet creates an EVM key and a Solana keypair in one command. Both encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM + scrypt, derived per-user from a server master secret.

  2. Step 02

    Fund + set your policy

    Send USDC (+ a little native gas) to either address. /settings tunes your caps — $ per trade, daily spend limit, cooldown, slippage, chain lock.

  3. Step 03

    Watch a wallet or DCA

    /watch 0x... for copy-trading, or /dca to buy on a schedule. Mirror flow and DCA flow share one buyAssetWithUsdc() primitive — same policy gate on every path.

  4. Step 04

    Onchain execution

    Bot fetches a quote from Zerion's /swap/offers, approves USDC if needed, signs the tx locally with viem (EVM) or ed25519 (Solana), and broadcasts. Real tx hash in ~15 seconds.

Scoped policies

Every swap, every DCA tick, gated.

All paths funnel through one checkPolicy() call. Rejections carry a clear reason and surface in the audit table. No bypass, no god-mode agent.
